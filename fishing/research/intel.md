## 2. Analysis

### 2a. What is being caught where RIGHT NOW (10 Oct - 21 Nov 2026)

Observation summary (all sources together, ~45 dated items, only ~25 of them actual rod catches):
- **Bass (shore): strongest signal.** MBAS (Penzance club, venue withheld) logged shore bass of 3120, 3068/3026, 3060, 2320, 2150, 2140, 2110, 1850, 1700, 1690, 1420 g between 18 Oct and 16 Nov. Latest: **two bass 3060 g + 2110 g on the night of 16 Nov**, 6 days before the session. TSF W Cornwall: estuary "stacked with bass ... nothing big" 30 Oct. Gilbey: estuary bass ~5 lb c.22-23 Oct. Commercial handliners in Mount's Bay/SW approaches "busy on the bass" 9 Nov and "the bass men are enjoying a few good fish" over 14-15 Nov (TTG, boat). By size, 2026 is the best MBAS autumn for bass in four years (bass competition top three 3120/3068/2140 g against a 2595 g winner in 2023).
- **Wrasse:** still feeding when the rocks can be fished. 6 Nov: 7 fish on a bass lure on an open W Cornwall mark in big swell. MBAS ballan 1255 g on 5 Nov. W Cornwall estuary/river fish on lures on 20 Nov (species unstated). The main specialist says he has barely been able to target them for ~6 weeks because of swell.
- **Pollack (shore):** MBAS 3120 g (2 Nov), 2555 g (5 Nov), 1245 g (7 Nov). Steady pollack landings at Newlyn as well.
- **Small-eyed ray / bull huss / rockling / LSD:** MBAS small-eyed ray 2360 g and bull huss 4100 g (19 Oct); three-bearded rockling 570 g (2 Nov); LSD won the 1 Nov rover with 1130 g.
- **Pelagic predators and bait close in:** bluefin tuna seen from shore at Kilgerran Head (7 Nov, 10 fish), Mount's Bay from Trenow (8 Nov), Bedruthan Steps (8 Nov), East Pentire (11 Nov, "Tuna"). Common dolphins Pendeen-Geevor (13 Nov), and 25+ on the Penzance-Wolf Rock crossing (7 Nov). Newlyn sardine boats report bluefin and dolphins "chasing the shoals hard" (8-9 Nov). Large feeding flocks: Levant >1 mile long on 15 Oct, Porthkerris 500 gannets on 12 Oct. Since 13 Nov there are **no** tuna, dolphin or feeding-flock records on the mainland or in Scilly (Scilly weekly log 12-18 Nov is blank for marine life). Weather was poor 18-19 Nov, so this may only be fewer observers (THIN DATA).
- **Squid:** jigged from small boats 17-18 Oct, before Storm Austen (22-23 Oct), and 9 Nov (Bethshan "got in amongst them"). No shore squid report.
- **Mackerel / red mullet / gilthead:** boat landings of good-size mackerel 9 Nov, red mullet 9 and 16 Nov, gilthead bream 5-8 Nov (all TTG, boat). No shore reports.
- **Conditions pattern:** Storm Austen (NW) on 22-23 Oct, "truly filthy" weather on 26 Oct, easterlies around 30 Oct, big swell on 6 Nov, colder from 8 Nov, mild on 16 Nov, fleet tied up 18-19 Nov, and still swell on the W Cornwall rocks on 20 Nov. Weed was heavy at Perranuthnoe on 3 Oct.

### 2c. Strongest and weakest signals
Strongest (multiple independent sources, verified):
1. Big bass are still being caught from the shore in West Cornwall at night into mid-Nov (MBAS 16 Nov, grade B, photographed). Boat bass reported by TTG in the same days corroborates this.
2. Bluefin and dolphins were hunting baitfish within sight of both coasts on 7-13 Nov (4 shore-based CBWPS observers on both coasts, plus the TTG market report).
