## Evidence summary and warnings
- The sector is very thinly documented online. About 80% of per-mark text comes from Where's The Fish (wheresthefish.uk) site-generated descriptions (grade C; underlying observations not stated, apparently model-written, includes season/species rosters that are generic). Treat unsourced species lists as D (inference).
- Genuinely independent evidence: British Sea Fishing South West page (B, undated), FISHMAG Mevagissey/Cornwall guides (C, pre-2020), one NESA 2013 St Catherine's Point report (C), one 2018 Polruan blog (C), TSF Par thread 2024 (C), CFSA recorder reports 2024/2025 (A, but NO venue named), WtF catch log Par/Charlestown Oct/Sep 2026 (C).
- CFSA reports: Mevagissey SAC members took specimen shore fish in late 2024: Nov 2024 scad 1-4-6 (CFSA 127.31 pct, shore fish of the month, senior) and Nov 2024 ladies bass 7-15-0 (99.23 pct); Jan 2024 ladies whiting 1-13-0; Jun 2024 gilthead bream 3-12-6; 2025 ladies thick-lip mullet 3-5-3 (Jun), 4-10-0 (Aug), 4-0-3 (Sep), bass 4-7-0 (Oct), and a new CFSA shore record thin-lipped mullet 3-3-4 (2025). Venues are NOT stated; plausibly Mevagissey/Fowey area but cannot be pinned (D).
- No source gives a late-November catch report for any named S4 mark. Late-November lists below are D inference from generic winter statements unless marked.
- Fishmag (C, pre-2020) states winter fishing is better at Fowey from dusk than at Mevagissey, but Mevagissey still produces dogfish and winter squid.
- Contradictions: Fowey pontoon rod fishing is prohibited per National Trust/Harbour Commissioners yet blogs/FB report pontoon fishing (OPP 2018 fly; FB Albert Quay pontoon) - irrelevant to fish quality, noted only. Par docks 'private/closed' (search summary) vs TSF/FMC saying access by permit/club.
- Not found: warm-water outfall history at Par (searched; nothing), any record listing naming an S4 venue, Sea Angler/Total Sea Fishing article bodies (paywalled), Fishbrain mark lists.

Sources key: wtf = Where's The Fish; BSF = britishseafishing.co.uk; FMC/FM = fishmag.co.uk; KF = kayakfishing.blog; NESA = nesa.co.uk thread; OPP = opportunisticanglers blog; TSF = talkseafishing forum; CF = CFSA recorders.
