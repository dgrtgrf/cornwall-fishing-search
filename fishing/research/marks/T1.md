## Cross-cutting evidence (observation vs inference)
- OBS (C, search snippets, not fetched in full): big winter flounder strongly associated with Devon/Cornwall estuaries; run starts October, accelerates Nov-Dec, peaks January; peeler crab best, ragworm/lug also; most fish within 30-40 yd.
- OBS (B, britishseafishing.co.uk South West guide): winter whiting/flounder/cod in Looe river; flounder, mullet, school bass up the Fowey; flounder and bass Pentewan/St Austell Bay; bass, flounder, cod, whiting and sea trout up the Tamar.
- OBS (C, lovelycornwall/cornwall-online): Looe and Fowey hold very large flounder; best on an incoming tide on a winter night; pollock up Looe River under the fish market on cold winter nights.
- OBS (B, Helford MCG): cod, whiting, flounder, thornback, bass, mullet, gilthead listed, season dependent, no months; shore rod-and-line subject to Duchy consent.
- OBS (A, BRFC June 2026): UK shore flounder 5-07-00 Barry Sokell, River Teign (Devon) 1994; boat flounder 5-11-08 A G L Cobbledick, Fowey 1956. No Cornish shore record found.
- NOT FOUND: any Cornish estuary gilthead report for late November (only summer/autumn; spawning Nov-Dec per Cornwall Good Seafood Guide, no catch implication). D: unlikely in the shallows by 22 Nov.
- NOT FOUND: Cornish estuary thornback/conger/huss/eel/sea-trout reports with mark names (Devil's Point conger/cod/thornie is Devon side, Gilbey C).
- NOT FOUND: per-mark freshwater-flush, spring/neap and state-of-tide behaviour. Generic only: flounder/bass move on the flood; Tresillian fishes only at HW (forum C); mullet follow rising tide (Helford MCG B). Heavy-rain behaviour is D inference only.
- Disagreement: Helford bass minimum 42 cm (MCG) vs 37.5 cm from 2009 (search snippet); grey mullet minimum 30 cm vs 20 cm in two IFCA snippets. Not central; check Cornwall IFCA.
- D for 22 Nov: flounder/whiting on the flood and at night plausible; bass, mullet, bream weaker as water cools. No tide or rain data fetched by this stream.
