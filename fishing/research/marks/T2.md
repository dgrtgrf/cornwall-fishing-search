## Cross-cutting findings

- **Squid (late November):** Fishing News (fishingnews.co.uk, Phil Lockley "Squid diaries"; page itself 403 to fetch, only search-snippet seen, grade C): November is "the very best time" for squid in Falmouth Bay, "mid-November" peak, biggest late-Nov 2013 squid 2.6 kg; this is BOAT jigging. Shore evidence for Cornish harbours is THIN DATA: a WSF Falmouth thread names Falmouth docks lights as a squid spot and says darkness is not needed; a WSF thread says squid fishing in west Cornwall was "September-October" and another poster says Dec-Jan best, with the tail of November fishing well in cold rough weather. Wheresthefish ranks Squid in the top 3 for ~20 harbour marks for 21-27 Nov (its own algorithm; not evidence).
- **Lights:** Falmouth docks and Falmouth waterfront have the best squid-under-lights suggestion (C). No light data found for Newlyn, Penzance, Porthleven, Looe, Fowey.
- **Winter fish at harbour marks (consistent across sources):** whiting, pouting, flounder (inside harbours and creeks), some cod/codling (BSF: Penzance, Polperro/Looe, Fowey), conger at night (Newlyn pier, Falmouth docks, Coverack, Porthleven rare), small pollack, rockling, herring on LRF (Penzance), thornback ray (Falmouth PoW Pier). Late mackerel: one mid-December fish at PoW Pier (forum).
- **Depth alongside at LW/HW:** NOT FOUND for every mark in metres. Not obtained from any harbour authority chart (no source fetched; UKHO charts not freely available).
- **Swell surge / coloured water effects:** NOT FOUND in any source for these marks; Mullion harbour blog warns waves break over the outer arm (presentation: only short end section fishable); Falmouth forum poster: heavy rain may put fish off (C). Everything else is inference (D): sheltered ria/estuary marks (Falmouth, Fowey, Looe, Mylor) are insulated from Atlantic swell; open harbours (Bude, Sennen, Newlyn South Pier, Porthleven, Mullion, Portreath) take surge.
- **Conger specimen history:** no verifiable shore conger record found for any harbour. 20 lb eel from Penzance lighthouse pier (forum hearsay); UK shore conger record 68 lb 8 oz Devil's Point, Plymouth 1992 (search snippet, Devon not Cornwall).
- **LRF/mini-species:** Penzance inner basin (gobies), Looe Banjo Pier (gobies, blennies, small wrasse year-round), Porthleven outer harbour (gobies, wrasse) per forum and site text.
- **Source quirk:** BSF text says "Banjo Pier in Polperro" - almost certainly Looe; treat as BSF error (D).
