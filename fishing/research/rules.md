### 1.2 Bass nursery areas (BNAs) touching Cornwall

Instrument: **The Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) Order 1990, SI 1990/1156**, as varied by **The Bass (Specified Areas) (Prohibition of Fishing) (Variation) Order 1999, SI 1999/75** (in force 15 Feb 1999; substitutes the whole Schedule and adds the sandeel-bait prohibition). Fetched from https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1990/1156/made and https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1999/75/made at 15:19 UTC (grade A).

Isles of Scilly: no bass nursery area is designated in the Order (none of the 37 Schedule entries is in Scilly).

What this means for a SHORE angler (observation from the Order's own words, grade A; interpretation grade D):
- The prohibition is on fishing "by any fishing boat". It does not, on its face, prohibit an angler standing on the shore from fishing for bass inside a BNA, or from using sandeel as bait from the shore. Cornwall IFCA's 2026 leaflet words it the same way: areas "where fishing for bass **from a boat** is prohibited, and also where fishing **from a boat** for any species of sea fish using sand eels as bait is prohibited". MMO guidance s.2.5: "... by any fishing boat within any part of the areas".
- Therefore on 22 Nov 2026 a shore angler in the Camel, Fowey, Fal (above Turnaware), Percuil, Helford or Tamar/Lynher may legally fish for bass with any bait including sandeel, and may retain up to 3 bass of 42 cm+ (the national Art. 10 rule). From a boat inside those lines on that date, bass fishing and any use of sandeel bait is prohibited.
- Inference (D): because the study's "superhuman shore angler" stands where land meets water, the BNA Order never bites on the chosen method. It would bite if the angler waded? No - wading is still not "a fishing boat".
- Voluntary: angling bodies (e.g. BASS, Angling Trust) encourage catch-and-release of bass in nursery estuaries; not law. (B - not individually re-verified in this session; flagged THIN.)
